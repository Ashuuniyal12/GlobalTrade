# Global Trading & Export Website — Architecture

> **Purpose:** Build a professional, fast, mobile-first B2B website for a global trading, import/export, shipping, and customs-focused business. The site should generate qualified WhatsApp and email enquiries and support organic search visibility, especially for pharmaceutical and API-related trade keywords.
>
> **Important positioning rule:** Do not present the company as exclusively pharmaceutical. Pharmaceuticals and APIs are priority SEO/service areas, while the overall brand remains accessible to textiles, raw materials, industrial goods, and other legitimate sectors. Do not invent certifications, client logos, shipment volumes, offices, years of experience, partner networks, or service capabilities.

---

## 1. Business goals

1. Build trust with international buyers, suppliers, importers, exporters, and logistics partners.
2. Rank for relevant pharmaceutical/API import-export and customs-related searches, without keyword stuffing or guarantees of ranking.
3. Convert visitors into enquiries through prominent WhatsApp and email actions and a clear enquiry form.
4. Explain the company’s actual services, industries, and supported trade corridors.
5. Deliver a fast, accessible, responsive experience on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
6. Make content easy to update and expand as the business develops.

## 2. Brand and design direction

**Brand personality:** Professional, dependable, international, precise, approachable.

**Visual direction:**
- Premium B2B corporate design; avoid generic template styling and excessive animation.
- Deep navy, white/off-white, and a restrained teal or muted gold accent.
- Suggested palette: `#102A43` navy, `#176B87` teal, `#F4F7FA` off-white, `#D6A85F` accent.
- Use a legible modern typeface such as Inter or Manrope.
- Use authentic, licensed, optimized photography for cargo, ports, international trade, pharmaceutical manufacturing, APIs, textiles, and industrial goods.
- Avoid using pharmaceutical imagery in every section; the company serves multiple industries.
- Use generous whitespace, clear hierarchy, concise copy, consistent cards, subtle borders, and restrained shadows.
- Use Framer Motion only for subtle entrance transitions and small interactions. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Do not use fake counters, unsupported “global leader” claims, fabricated testimonials, or decorative elements that obstruct content.

**Suggested homepage message:**
- Eyebrow: `GLOBAL TRADE • IMPORT & EXPORT • CUSTOMS SUPPORT`
- H1: `Connecting Global Markets with Confidence`
- Supporting text: `Import/export and logistics coordination backed by a practical understanding of customs procedures and cross-border trade requirements.`
- Primary CTA: `Discuss Your Shipment`
- Secondary CTA: `Explore Our Services`

Copy must be reviewed and confirmed by the client before publication. Avoid implying that the company itself is a licensed customs broker, freight carrier, pharmaceutical manufacturer, or regulatory authority unless confirmed.

## 4. Homepage section order

1. **Header:** Logo, Services, Industries, Global Markets, About, Contact; visible `Request a Quote` CTA. Mobile menu must be keyboard accessible.
2. **Hero:** Strong trade-focused H1, short benefit statement, primary CTA and secondary CTA, high-quality shipping/logistics image. Include WhatsApp as a secondary contact option rather than making the hero crowded.
3. **Trust/value strip:** 3–4 verified strengths such as customs-focused support, cross-industry capability, clear communication, and international trade coordination. Do not show unverified statistics.
4. **Services:** Trade coordination, customs documentation/procedure support, freight/shipping coordination, and import/export support—only services confirmed by the client.
5. **Priority sector feature:** Pharmaceuticals and APIs, describing the actual services and applicable compliance boundaries.
6. **Other industries:** Textiles, raw materials, industrial goods, consumer/commercial goods, and “other sectors.”
7. **Global markets:** Map or route graphic illustrating confirmed trade corridors. Do not imply that every country or route is currently serviced.
8. **How it works:** Share requirements → review shipment/trade needs → confirm scope and documentation → coordinate agreed next steps.
9. **Why work with us:** Specific, verifiable business practices rather than unsupported superlatives.
10. **FAQ:** Questions about services, supported markets, quote requests, documentation, and regulated goods. Answers must be checked by the client.
11. **Final CTA:** `Tell Us About Your Shipment` with WhatsApp and email options.
12. **Footer:** Company name, verified contact details, navigation, service links, privacy policy, social links only if real, and business address only if approved.

## 5. Pharmaceutical and API SEO strategy

### SEO objective
Build topical relevance and useful landing pages for pharmaceutical/API-related trade searches while maintaining a broad global trade brand. SEO is ongoing work; rankings cannot be guaranteed by implementation alone.

### Keyword themes to research and validate
Use Search Console, keyword research tools, customer language, and actual service capabilities to validate target phrases. Initial themes may include:
- pharmaceutical export services
- pharmaceutical import export company
- API export services
- active pharmaceutical ingredients exporter
- pharmaceutical logistics and shipping
- pharmaceutical customs documentation
- API import export documentation
- international pharmaceutical trade support
- pharmaceutical shipment coordination
- import/export documentation for pharmaceutical products

Use location modifiers only where the company truly operates or serves customers. Do not target “manufacturer,” “wholesaler,” “supplier,” “GMP certified,” or similar terms unless they accurately describe the company.

### On-page requirements
For every indexable page:
- One descriptive, unique H1.
- Unique title tag (aim for roughly 50–60 characters where practical; prioritize clarity over a rigid count).
- Unique meta description (often around 140–160 characters; not a direct ranking guarantee).
- Clear URL slug in lowercase, using hyphens.
- Natural use of the primary topic and related terms in the title, H1, intro, subheadings, body, internal links, and image alt text where relevant.
- Useful content that answers buyer questions, explains scope, clarifies the company’s role, and provides a clear CTA.
- Relevant internal links to services, industry pages, global markets, and contact.
- Descriptive image filenames and accurate alt text; decorative images should have empty alt text.
- Canonical URL for each indexable page.
- Open Graph and Twitter/X metadata.
- Breadcrumbs on deeper pages.
- Avoid keyword stuffing, hidden text, doorway pages, duplicate pages, and automatically generated low-value content.

### Pharmaceutical/API page content guidance
Each priority landing page should clarify:
- What the company actually provides: trade coordination, sourcing, export/import assistance, documentation support, shipping coordination, or other confirmed services.
- Product scope, only at the level the company can reliably support.
- Supported origin/destination markets, once confirmed.
- High-level documentation and compliance considerations, with a clear note that requirements depend on product, origin, destination, and transaction.
- A clear enquiry CTA.
- FAQs based on real client questions.
- Relevant related service links.

Do not provide legal/regulatory assurances such as “guaranteed customs clearance” or claim that all APIs/pharmaceutical products can be shipped everywhere. Regulated goods may require permits, licenses, quality documentation, special handling, or other country-specific checks. Ask the client to have regulatory statements reviewed by a qualified professional.

### Content plan for future SEO
Create useful, expert-reviewed resources only when the company can verify the information, for example:
- A practical overview of documents commonly involved in pharmaceutical exports
- Questions to clarify before arranging an API shipment
- How import/export documentation varies by destination
- How to prepare an international shipment enquiry
- General differences between freight coordination and customs clearance

Articles must be original, current, useful, and reviewed. Add publication/update dates when meaningful. Do not publish AI-generated regulatory guidance without fact-checking.

## 6. Technical SEO implementation

Use Next.js App Router and the Metadata API.

Implement:
- `metadata` exports for static pages.
- `generateMetadata()` for dynamic pages/resources.
- `metadataBase` configured with the confirmed production domain.
- Canonical URLs.
- Open Graph metadata and `twitter` card metadata.
- `app/robots.ts` for robots rules.
- `app/sitemap.ts` for the XML sitemap.
- A custom not-found page.
- Clean redirects if URLs change.
- Structured data using JSON-LD for applicable schema types:
  - `Organization` or the most accurate organization subtype.
  - `WebSite`.
  - `BreadcrumbList` on nested pages.
  - `Article` for editorial resources.
  - `Service` where appropriate and factually supported.
- Do not add fabricated reviews, ratings, prices, awards, addresses, or business hours to schema.
- Do not assume FAQ rich results will appear just because FAQ schema is present.
- Use absolute production URLs in canonical tags and structured data.
- Exclude staging, preview, private, duplicate, and non-indexable URLs from the production sitemap.
- Do not block assets required for rendering in `robots.txt`.
- Verify the domain in Google Search Console and submit the sitemap after launch.
- Add analytics and conversion events only with appropriate privacy/consent handling.

## 7. Conversion design: WhatsApp, email, and form

### WhatsApp
- Add a visible but unobtrusive WhatsApp CTA on desktop and mobile.
- Use a configurable phone number in international format, stored in one site configuration file or environment variable.
- Use a prefilled message such as: `Hello, I would like to discuss an international shipment/trade enquiry.`
- Encode the prefilled message correctly for a `wa.me` URL.
- Do not invent a WhatsApp number.
- Add `aria-label` text and ensure the floating button does not cover form controls, cookie notices, or mobile navigation.
- Track CTA clicks if analytics is configured with the required consent.

### Email
- Use the confirmed business email address from a central configuration.
- Add a clear `mailto:` link and display the email in the contact/footer section.
- Do not invent an email address.

### Enquiry form
Fields:
- Name (required)
- Business email (required; validate format)
- Company (optional unless client requests otherwise)
- Country (optional or required according to business needs)
- Enquiry type (trade, pharmaceutical/API, customs documentation, freight, other)
- Origin and destination (optional)
- Product/goods description (required only if appropriate)
- Message (required)
- Consent/privacy acknowledgement where applicable

Requirements:
- Clear labels, inline validation, useful error messages, loading state, success state, and failure state.
- Protect against spam using server-side validation, rate limiting, and a honeypot or appropriate CAPTCHA if needed.
- Do not expose email credentials, API keys, or secrets to the browser.
- If a backend email provider is configured, send the enquiry server-side.
- Never silently discard a submission. Show a clear confirmation only after the configured handler succeeds.
- Do not claim a message was sent if delivery has not been confirmed by the application.
- If there is no backend configured in the initial MVP, provide WhatsApp/email alternatives and clearly document the form as needing configuration before production.

## 8. Technology stack

### Required
- Next.js 15+ with App Router (use a currently supported stable version compatible with the deployment target).
- TypeScript with strict mode.
- Tailwind CSS.
- Framer Motion for minimal motion.
- shadcn/ui components, customized to the brand.
- Next.js Metadata API.
- `app/sitemap.ts` and `app/robots.ts`.
- Responsive image handling with `next/image` where compatible with the selected hosting/runtime.

### Suggested supporting libraries
- `lucide-react` for icons.
- `react-hook-form` and `zod` for form validation, if needed.
- A server-side email service such as Resend, SMTP through a trusted provider, or the client's existing mail service—choose after confirming credentials and hosting support.
- Optional analytics only after privacy requirements are agreed.

Avoid adding libraries without a clear need. Keep the initial site mostly static/server-rendered to reduce JavaScript and improve performance.

## 10. Configuration and environment

Keep business details centralized in `src/config/site.ts`, for example:
- company display name
- production site URL
- short description
- business email
- WhatsApp phone
- business address (only if approved)
- social profile URLs (only real profiles)
- supported markets
- confirmed services
- logo and brand assets

Use `.env.example` to document required environment variables without real secrets. Never commit `.env.local`, API keys, email credentials, or private client data.

Potential environment variables:
```bash
NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL=https://example.com
NEXT_PUBLIC_WHATSAPP_NUMBER=
CONTACT_EMAIL=
EMAIL_PROVIDER_API_KEY=
```

Replace `example.com` and blank values with client-approved settings. Only values safe for browser exposure should use the `NEXT_PUBLIC_` prefix. `CONTACT_EMAIL` and provider keys should remain server-side unless there is a specific reason otherwise.

## 11. Performance and accessibility

Targets:
- Aim for Core Web Vitals in the “Good” range on real users; do not claim a score until measured.
- Target Lighthouse performance/accessibility/SEO best practices of 90+ where practical, without sacrificing usability or content quality.
- Use optimized WebP/AVIF images and explicit image dimensions.
- Prioritize the hero image appropriately; lazy-load below-the-fold imagery.
- Avoid autoplay background video, oversized libraries, excessive client components, and heavy animation.
- Prefer Server Components; use Client Components only for interaction.
- Use semantic HTML, logical headings, visible focus indicators, keyboard navigation, sufficient color contrast, and descriptive form errors.
- Support reduced motion.
- Ensure touch targets are comfortable on mobile.
- Test mobile menu, WhatsApp links, email links, all forms, and footer navigation on small screens.

## 12. Hostinger Business Hosting deployment considerations

**Do not assume standard Hostinger shared Business web hosting can run every Next.js deployment mode.** Before implementation, confirm the exact hosting product, plan, Node.js/Next.js support, and supported deployment mode.

Deployment decision:
1. Check the current Hostinger plan documentation and account capabilities.
2. If the plan explicitly supports the required Next.js version and server runtime, deploy using the supported Node.js application process and follow Hostinger's current instructions.
3. If only static hosting is supported, a static export may be used only if the site is designed to work without server-only features. Server-side enquiry API routes, request-time rendering, and some dynamic features will not work in a pure static export.
4. If server-side form handling is required and the hosting plan cannot support it, use a supported external serverless/API service or choose a compatible hosting tier. Keep the main domain and email DNS configuration coordinated.
5. Test the production build and all enquiry flows on the actual hosting target before launch.

Do not silently switch to a static export or remove server functionality to make deployment pass. Document the chosen deployment architecture.

## 13. Security and privacy

- Validate all form inputs on the server, even if client-side validation exists.
- Sanitize and safely render user-provided content.
- Add rate limiting/spam protection to public endpoints.
- Keep secrets server-side.
- Use HTTPS and secure provider configuration.
- Collect only enquiry information the business needs.
- Publish a privacy policy reviewed for the company's actual practices and jurisdictions.
- Set analytics/cookie consent behavior according to applicable requirements.
- Do not request confidential formulas, unnecessary regulated product details, or sensitive documents through an unsecured form.

## 14. Analytics and conversion measurement

If approved and configured, track:
- WhatsApp CTA clicks
- Email CTA clicks
- Enquiry form starts
- Successful form submissions
- Form validation/failure events
- Primary CTA clicks

Do not count a form submission as successful merely because the submit button was clicked. Avoid collecting unnecessary personal data in analytics.

## 17. AI implementation instructions

Build the website in small, verifiable stages:

1. Inspect the repository and existing files before changing anything. Preserve working functionality.
2. Confirm the installed Node.js/package-manager versions and the actual Hostinger deployment constraints.
3. Set up or refine the Next.js App Router, strict TypeScript, Tailwind, and shadcn/ui.
4. Implement the shared layout, brand tokens, navigation, and footer.
5. Build the homepage with reusable, responsive sections.
6. Add the services, industries, global markets, about, and contact pages with distinct content.
7. Implement central site configuration, metadata helpers, JSON-LD, sitemap, robots, and canonical handling.
8. Implement WhatsApp/email CTAs and the validated enquiry form. Do not pretend delivery works until a real server-side handler is configured and tested.
9. Optimize images, motion, accessibility, and loading behavior.
10. Run lint, type checks, production build, link checks, and form tests. Fix all errors introduced by the implementation.
11. Test the actual production deployment target, especially form submission and server-side features.

**Do not stop at a mockup.** Deliver working pages and interactions, but do not invent client facts or credentials. If a required value is unknown, centralize it as a clearly documented configuration placeholder and list it in the handover. Do not claim SEO rankings or performance scores without measurement.
